About the project

"Old Skopje" is a multimedia CD-ROM published in 2001. This website restores its content: all the photographs, texts, films, the narrated chronology and the music are taken directly from the original disc.

This CD was made by Veselin Stefanovski

all copyrights belong to Veselin Stefanovski

restored by Bojan Božinovski
